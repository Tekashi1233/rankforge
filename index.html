Why RankForge

Most SEO tools ask you to sign up, connect your site, or pay before they tell you anything useful. RankForge works the other way around. Every tool on this page runs in your browser, on your device, the moment you open it. You paste your text or your URL, you get the answer, and nothing you typed is sent anywhere. That is the whole deal: free tools, no account, no uploads.

The tools cover the jobs that come up again and again when you publish a page. You can check a title tag and meta description with a live Google preview, build a sitemap or a robots.txt file, add valid schema markup, and see how your headings are ordered. You can also check how your content reads to the new answer engines: the GEO Score Checker grades a page for AI search, and the AI Readiness Checker shows where a page is thin on the facts and structure those systems look for.

If you want to understand the ideas behind the checks, the blog explains them in plain English. The guides walk through keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, and the newer GEO work, with examples you can copy. Start with any tool below, or read a guide first and come back when you have a page to test.
