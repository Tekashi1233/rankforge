## What was added and why

### Tool: Headline Analyzer
- URL: https://rankforgetool.com/tools/headline-analyzer
- File: tools/headline-analyzer.html

Why this tool: of the candidates, Headline Analyzer was the most distinct from the 16 existing tools. A Character Counter would have overlapped heavily with the Word Counter, which already counts words, characters, sentences and reading time. A Reading Time Calculator and a Meta Title Length Checker would have overlapped the same way. A Text Summarizer is hard to do well in plain client-side JavaScript. Headline Analyzer fits the site's SEO focus, people search for headline help a lot, and the scoring logic can be kept transparent and honest: it is RankForge's own checklist, not a claim about Google's algorithm. The score is 0-100 from length (30), word count (15), power/emotional words (20), number or question (15), and clarity (20), with every rule explained on the page.
