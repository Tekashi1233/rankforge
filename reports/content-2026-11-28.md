## What was added and why

### Tool: Alt Text Checker
- URL: https://rankforgetool.com/tools/alt-text-checker
- File: tools/alt-text-checker.html

Why this tool: owner-picked. It fills a real gap: no existing tool touches image accessibility. The closest neighbours are the Readability Score Checker (plain writing, a different job) and the Headline Analyzer (short text, a different job). Every page with images needs alt text, and the 125-character guideline is something people really search for. Runs fully in plain browser JavaScript: describe the image and get a suggested alt (strips "image of"/"photo of", capitalises, trims to 125 characters at a word boundary), or paste alt text you wrote and get a 0-100 score plus per-check verdicts: length against the 125-character guideline, "image of" opener, filename pattern, repeated-word stuffing, "click here", all capitals, thin alt, linked-image destination, decorative-image handling (empty alt is correct, non-empty alt flagged). Copy/clear/example buttons, checks run live as you type.
