## What was added and why

### Tool: Base64 Encoder / Decoder
- URL: https://rankforgetool.com/tools/base64-encoder-decoder
- File: tools/base64-encoder-decoder.html

Why this tool: suggested in the 2026-11-13 report as the strongest of the three candidates. It is the most distinct from the 19 existing tools: the closest neighbour is the URL Encoder / Decoder, but the two do different jobs (percent-encoding a URL vs writing binary data as text). People meet Base64 constantly (email attachments, login headers, tokens, data URLs) and search for a way to read it. The whole thing runs in plain browser JavaScript with no server: encode/decode in standard and URL-safe variants, UTF-8 handling via TextEncoder/TextDecoder (so Arabic and emoji survive, unlike bare btoa()/atob()), live as-you-type, character counts with size change, error message on invalid input, whitespace-tolerant decode, missing-padding repair, copy/clear/example buttons.
