## What was found

### Critical

None. No broken pages, no missing trust pages, no invalid XML or JSON-LD, no missing
titles, descriptions, canonicals or H1s. The live home page, sitemap, RSS feed, robots.txt,
one tool page (`/tools/word-counter`) and one blog post
(`/blog/what-is-heading-structure-and-how-to-check-it`) all returned HTTP 200, and the
external links (Google Fonts, Google Policies) also returned 200.

### Important

1. **Sitemap incomplete and dateless (fixed).** `sitemap.xml` listed 44 of 47 live pages —
   `/tools/backlink-checker`, `/tools/rank-tracker` and `/tools/site-audit` were missing —
   and no entry had a `<lastmod>` date.
2. **RSS feed stale and incomplete (fixed).** The newest post (heading structure, published
   5 Nov 2026) was missing from `rss.xml`, and 7 of the 19 items had `pubDate` 1 Oct 2026
   while the articles' own `datePublished` is 31 Oct – 4 Nov 2026. `lastBuildDate` was 5 Nov.
3. **Thin blog content (partly fixed, mostly open).** 11 of 20 posts are under ~800 words
   counting the whole page (article text alone is shorter): heading structure ~370 words,
   URL slugs ~592, title case ~631, Open Graph ~632, readability ~635, LLMs ~662, how many
   words ~658, strong password ~696, lorem ipsum ~714, robots.txt ~732, meta description ~752.
   The heading-structure post was expanded to ~537 words (see fixes). The other 10 still need
   substantive original expansion — that is writing work, not a safe small fix.
4. **Three tool pages are placeholders, not tools (open — owner decision needed).**
   `/tools/backlink-checker`, `/tools/rank-tracker` and `/tools/site-audit` are honest
   "coming soon" pages with `noindex,follow`, no working tool, and no how-to/FAQ. They are
   also not listed on `/tools` or the home page. Building them, or removing the pages, is a
   product decision. Note: this audit added them to the sitemap because every live page
   should be listed, but they are `noindex` — see "Not fixed" below.
5. **Long meta descriptions (fixed).** 6 pages had descriptions over 160 characters:
   home 172, GEO Score Checker 217, Heading Structure Checker 163, LLMs post 172,
   GEO 2027 post 188, readability post 168. Google truncates these in snippets.
6. **Missing social tags (fixed).** The three coming-soon pages had no `og:type`,
   `og:site_name`, `og:locale`, image dimensions, Twitter card tags, RSS link or JSON-LD.
   The AI Readiness Checker had no Twitter card tags and no `og:image` dimensions.
7. **Heading skips on trust pages (fixed).** About, Privacy and Terms jumped from H1
   straight to H3 for every section (5, 9 and 8 headings respectively).
8. **One working tool lacked how-to and FAQ (fixed).** The Heading Structure Checker had
   an explanation and a "common problems" list but no "How to use" section and no FAQ,
   unlike the other 14 working tools. It also had no link to its guide post.
9. **Misleading structured data on the home page (fixed).** The `WebSite` JSON-LD
   advertised a `SearchAction` pointing at `/tools`, but the site has no search function.

### Minor

- **11 pin images are orphaned.** `/pins/*.jpg` (100–130 KB each, all under 200 KB) exist
  for 11 posts but no blog post uses an `<img>` tag at all — the whole site has zero `<img>`
  elements, so there is no missing-alt-text problem, but also no article imagery.
- **No article images.** Related to the above; posts are text-only.
- **Google Fonts stylesheet is render-blocking.** Standard `<link rel="stylesheet">` to
  `fonts.googleapis.com` (with `display=swap`). Low impact; left as is.
- **No `Organization` JSON-LD on About/Contact.** The home page has `WebSite` +
  `Organization` blocks; the trust pages have none. Small E-E-A-T opportunity.
- **No author information.** Article JSON-LD lists the author as the Organization
  "RankForge"; there are no author names or bios anywhere.
- **Two audit false alarms, confirmed not real:** the "duplicate `<title>`" on the Meta Tag
  Generator is a `<title>` string inside the tool's own JavaScript output builder, and the
  "invalid JSON-LD" on the Schema Markup Generator is the same kind of artefact — a
  `application/ld+json` string inside the tool's JavaScript. A proper HTML parser confirms
  all real JSON-LD blocks on all 47 pages parse as valid JSON.
- **No broken internal links.** A check of every internal `href` found no dead page or
  asset targets (two apparent hits were JavaScript template fragments, not real links).
- **No duplicate titles, descriptions or canonicals** across the 47 pages.
- **No images over 200 KB.** Largest is 132 KB (`pins/what-is-keyword-density-and-how-to-use-keywords.jpg`).

## What was NOT fixed and why

1. **Thin content on 10 remaining posts.** Expanding a post properly needs 150–400 words of
   original, accurate writing per post. That is editorial work, not a safe mechanical fix.
   Recommended: expand 2–3 posts per week, starting with the thinnest (URL slugs, title case,
   Open Graph, readability). Do not pad with filler — AdSense reviewers penalise that.
2. **The three coming-soon tool pages.** Building a backlink checker, rank tracker or site
   audit as a real browser tool is a product decision, and deleting the pages would change
   URLs. They are honestly labelled, `noindex`, and not linked from Tools/Home, which is the
   correct holding pattern. Open question for the owner: the sitemap now lists them while
   they are `noindex` (Google tolerates this but may warn in Search Console). When a tool
   launches, remove its `noindex`; if a tool is abandoned, remove the page from the sitemap
   (and consider removing the page itself).
3. **Orphaned pin images / no article images.** The 11 `/pins/*.jpg` files match 11 posts
   but the site has no image component or CSS for article images, and no `<img>` anywhere.
   Adding images needs a small design decision (placement, sizing, `loading="lazy"`, alt text).
   Do not delete the pins — they are the intended artwork.
4. **Author/E-E-A-T information.** No author names or bios exist. Adding a real author
   (name, short bio, contact) needs the owner's input on who the author is.
5. **Render-blocking Google Fonts.** Standard practice, low impact; inlining or self-hosting
   the font is an optimisation for later, not a fix for this week.
6. **The report itself is not pushed.** Same credential reason as above; the report file
   exists at `/reports/audit-2026-11-06.md` in the local repo copy and at
   `~/workspace/reports/audit-2026-11-06.md`.
