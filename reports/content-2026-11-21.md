## What was added and why

### Tool: UTM Campaign Builder
- URL: https://rankforgetool.com/tools/utm-campaign-builder
- File: tools/utm-campaign-builder.html

Why this tool: owner-picked. It fills a real gap: no existing tool touches campaign tracking. The closest neighbours are the URL Encoder / Decoder (percent-encoding, a different job) and the Canonical URL Generator (clean canonical tags). People share links in newsletters, social posts and ads every day and cannot tell which one sent each visitor, so a builder that tags links correctly is something people really search for. Runs fully in plain browser JavaScript: destination URL plus utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign (required), utm_term / utm_content (optional), presets for Google Ads (google/cpc), Facebook (facebook/social), YouTube (youtube/social) and Email (newsletter/email), a lowercase-values switch (on by default, since Analytics treats values as case-sensitive), validation with plain error messages, old UTM parameters stripped and replaced instead of stacked, existing query strings and #fragments kept, copy/clear/example buttons, live as-you-type build.
