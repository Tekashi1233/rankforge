How to use this tool

  1. Enter your details. Use facts and wording that match the page visitors will see.
  2. Review the result. Check the preview or output and read any suggestions as editorial prompts.
  3. Copy or download. Take the generated result into your own site workflow and verify it before publishing.

RankNova processes the values in your current browser tab. The result is a starting point; it cannot inspect your website or guarantee a search display.

Tool FAQ

Common questions

Does the score predict clicks?

No. It is a lightweight writing check, not a traffic forecast. Audience, topic and distribution matter too.

What counts as a power word?

The analyzer checks a small set of vivid terms as a brainstorming cue. Context and truthful wording matter more than a list.

Should I always hit the highest score?

No. Keep the headline accurate and natural; ignore a suggestion if it makes the promise less clear.
