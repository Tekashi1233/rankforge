How to use the Slug Generator

Short, readable URLs are easier for people to trust and share. This tool removes accents and symbols, lowercases the text and joins words with hyphens, which is the separator search engines recommend. Drop filler words yourself for an even shorter slug.

Frequently asked questions

What is a URL slug?

The part of a web address that identifies a page, such as /on-page-seo-checklist.

Hyphens or underscores?

Use hyphens. Search engines treat hyphens as word separators, while underscores may join words together.

Should I include stop words like "the" or "and"?

Usually no. Shorter slugs are easier to read, as long as they still describe the page.
