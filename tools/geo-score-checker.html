What is a GEO Score?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of making content easier for AI answer engines — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and others — to find, understand and cite. A GEO Score is a simple 0–100 estimate of how well a piece of text is structured for those systems in 2027, when AI answers are expected to handle more than half of informational queries.

What this tool checks

  • Answer-first opening — Does the first 1–2 sentences give a clear, self-contained answer?
  • Statistics & numbers — Presence of specific figures, percentages or data points that engines like to quote.
  • Scannability — Short paragraphs, lists, and question-style language that help RAG systems extract passages.
  • Length balance — Enough substance to be useful without long walls of text.
  • Quotability signals — Clear statements that can stand alone when cited.

How to use the GEO Score Checker

1. Paste any section of content (a full post, a product description, an FAQ answer, or a knowledge-base article).
2. The score updates instantly as you type or paste.
3. Read the breakdown to see which factors are strong and which need work.
4. Improve the weak areas and re-check until the score is in a healthy range (aim for 70+).

Why this matters in 2027

By 2027, more than half of informational searches are expected to be answered directly by AI systems rather than a list of blue links. Content that is structured for extraction — clear answers up front, concrete data, short paragraphs, and quotable statements — is far more likely to appear in those answers. Traditional keyword density still helps discovery, but the final citation decision increasingly depends on how cleanly a passage can be lifted and attributed.

Frequently asked questions

Is a high GEO Score a guarantee of being cited?

No. The score is a heuristic based on observable text patterns. Authority, crawlability, freshness, and brand signals still matter. A high score simply means the text itself is structured in a way that answer engines prefer.

Does the tool send my content to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

What score should I aim for?

70 or higher is a solid target for most informational content. Scores above 85 usually mean the text is already very well structured for AI extraction.

Can I check an entire page?

Yes. Paste the main body text. The tool works best on the content that would actually be cited (skip navigation, footers and boilerplate).
