Frequently asked questions

What is title case?

A style where major words start with a capital letter, while small words such as "and", "of" and "the" stay lowercase unless they come first.

Is sentence case better for headings?

Both work. Pick one style and use it consistently across your site.

Does it keep my line breaks?

Yes. Only the letter case changes.
