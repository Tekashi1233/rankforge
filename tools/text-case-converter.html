What is a text case converter?

A text case converter changes the capitalization of letters in a block of text without altering the words themselves. Common options include UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case (also called headline style), and Sentence case. These tools save time when you need to reformat titles, headings, product names, or bulk text for consistency.

Why consistent capitalization matters

Consistent case improves professionalism and readability. Search engines do not rank pages higher simply because of title case versus sentence case, but clear, consistent styling helps users scan pages and builds brand trust. Title Case is traditional for many headlines and book titles; Sentence case feels more modern and conversational and is increasingly common on the web and in UI copy.

How to use this Text Case Converter

Paste or type any text, then click one of the four buttons:

  • UPPERCASE — Every letter becomes capital.
  • lowercase — Every letter becomes small.
  • Title Case — Major words are capitalized; small connecting words (a, an, the, and, of, etc.) stay lowercase unless they start the phrase.
  • Sentence case — Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalized.

Line breaks and punctuation are preserved. Copy the result with one click. Everything runs locally in your browser.

When to use each style

  • Title Case — Traditional headlines, book titles, product names, navigation labels.
  • Sentence case — Modern web headings, UI text, blog post titles that aim for a conversational tone.
  • UPPERCASE — Emphasis, short labels, or stylistic design choices (use sparingly for readability).
  • lowercase — Stylistic choice, usernames, or cleaning up accidentally capitalized text.

Best practices

  • Pick one primary style for headings across your site and stick to it.
  • Be consistent within the same page or document.
  • Avoid all-caps for long headings — it reduces readability and can feel like shouting.
  • When converting Title Case, double-check small words and proper nouns that the automatic rules may miss.

Frequently asked questions

What is title case?

A style where major words start with a capital letter, while small words such as "and", "of" and "the" stay lowercase unless they come first.

Is sentence case better for headings?

Both work. Pick one style and use it consistently across your site.

Does it keep my line breaks?

Yes. Only the letter case changes.

Does capitalization affect SEO?

Not directly. Search engines treat most case differences as the same words. Consistency and clarity for users matter more.
