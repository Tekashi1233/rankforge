What is lorem ipsum?


Lorem ipsum is the standard placeholder text used by designers, developers, and typographers when the real content is not yet available. It is a scrambled version of a passage from Cicero’s “De finibus bonorum et malorum” (45 BCE). Because the text looks roughly like natural language but carries no meaning, it lets people judge layout, spacing, and typography without being distracted by readable sentences.



When (and when not) to use placeholder text


Use lorem ipsum during the design and development stages: wireframes, mockups, theme demos, and client presentations. Replace it with real, useful content before the page goes live. Search engines and AI systems prefer original, helpful writing. Leaving “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…” on a published page is a common sign of an unfinished site and can hurt both user trust and SEO.

Frequently asked questions


What is lorem ipsum?
Placeholder text used to show how a layout looks before real content is ready.


Should I publish lorem ipsum?
No. Replace it with real content before your page goes live, since search engines prefer useful, original writing.


Why does the text start with the same phrase?
By tradition the first sentence is "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet". The rest is generated randomly each time.


Is lorem ipsum good for SEO testing?
No. Use real or realistic sample content when testing how search engines and AI tools interpret a page.
