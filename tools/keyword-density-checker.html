How to use the Keyword Density Checker

Paste your draft to see which words appear most. Your main topic word should show up naturally, but there is no magic percentage. If one word dominates, rewrite for readability instead of chasing a number.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good keyword density?

There is no magic percentage. Use your main keyword naturally and focus on writing helpful content instead.

Are common words counted?

Short filler words like "the" and "and" are ignored so the results show meaningful terms.

Is my text saved?

No. The analysis runs in your browser only.
