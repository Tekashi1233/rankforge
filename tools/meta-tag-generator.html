Frequently asked questions
What is the ideal title tag length?
Around 50 to 60 characters. Longer titles may be shortened in search results, though Google measures pixel width rather than a fixed number.
Does the meta description affect rankings?
Not directly. A clear description can earn more clicks, and clicks and engagement matter to your overall performance.
Do I need Open Graph tags?
They are optional for rankings, but they control how your page looks when shared on social networks and chat apps.
