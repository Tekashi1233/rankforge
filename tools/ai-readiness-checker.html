AI Readiness Checker

Paste your page's HTML (or just its text) to see how easy it is for AI search tools to understand and quote. Everything is checked in your browser.

How to use the AI Readiness Checker

Open your page, copy its HTML or text, paste it above, and press Check. You get a score and a list of fixes. The checks cover things that help both search engines and AI answer tools: a clear opening paragraph, good headings, lists, an FAQ section, structured data, image descriptions, enough depth, and whether your robots.txt blocks AI crawlers. A high score does not guarantee you will be quoted, but a page that fails many checks is harder for anyone to understand.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool guarantee I will appear in AI answers?

No. Nobody can guarantee that. It checks common good practices that make a page clearer and easier to quote.

Is my page uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.

Why does it ask for robots.txt?

If your robots.txt blocks AI crawlers, those tools cannot read your pages. The checker looks for full blocks on common AI crawlers.
