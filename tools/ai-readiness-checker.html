AI Readiness Checker

Paste your page's HTML (or just its text) to see how easy it is for AI search tools to understand and quote. Everything is checked in your browser.

What is AI readiness (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making web pages easy for AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, and others — to find, understand, and cite. Traditional SEO still matters, but AI systems also reward clear structure, direct answers, lists, FAQs, and structured data that help them extract reliable snippets.

Why checking AI readiness matters in 2026

A growing share of searches now ends with an AI-generated summary rather than a list of blue links. Pages that are well-structured, answer questions early, use descriptive headings, and allow AI crawlers are more likely to be quoted or linked. This free checker evaluates the most common signals that make a page easier for both search engines and generative tools to process.

How to use the AI Readiness Checker

1. Open the page you want to test and view its source (Ctrl+U / Cmd+Option+U) or simply copy the main text.
2. Paste the HTML or text into the first box.
3. Optionally paste your robots.txt to verify that AI crawlers are not blocked.
4. Click “Check my page.” You receive a readiness score and a checklist of strengths and suggested improvements.
5. All analysis runs locally — nothing is uploaded.

What the checker looks for

  • Clear page title and meta description
  • Exactly one H1 and sufficient subheadings
  • A direct opening paragraph that answers the main question
  • Lists, tables, or FAQ sections that are easy to quote
  • JSON-LD structured data
  • Image alt text and a canonical link
  • Sufficient content depth
  • robots.txt rules that do not fully block major AI crawlers

How to interpret the score

A high score means the page follows many current best practices for clarity and extractability. It does not guarantee that any particular AI tool will cite you. A low score highlights concrete, fixable issues that make the page harder for both humans and machines to understand quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool guarantee I will appear in AI answers?

No. Nobody can guarantee that. It checks common good practices that make a page clearer and easier to quote.

Is my page uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.

Why does it ask for robots.txt?

If your robots.txt blocks AI crawlers, those tools cannot read your pages. The checker looks for full blocks on common AI crawlers.

Is GEO different from SEO?

GEO builds on SEO. Classic technical and content SEO remain the foundation; GEO adds emphasis on structure, direct answers, and AI-crawler access.
