How to use the Password Generator

Pick a length and the character types you want, then tap Generate. Longer is stronger, so 16 characters or more is a good default. Passwords are created on your device and never sent or stored anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Are these passwords really random?

Yes. They use your browser's built-in cryptographic random number generator.

Is it safe to generate passwords online?

Here, yes, because nothing leaves your device. Still, store passwords in a reputable password manager.

How long should a password be?

At least 12 characters, and 16 or more is better. Use a different password for every account.
