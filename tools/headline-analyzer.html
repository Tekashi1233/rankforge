Headline Analyzer

Paste a headline to see its length, word balance and score.

What does a headline analyzer do?

A headline analyzer checks a headline before you publish it. It measures the character and word count, looks at the mix of common, uncommon, power and emotional words, and checks for a number, a question, all caps and extra punctuation. The result is a score out of 100, plus a short list of things to fix.

Why headlines matter for clicks and SEO

A headline is often the first thing a reader sees in search results or on social media. If it is unclear, too long or too vague, people skip past it, even when the page behind it is good. Google shows about 50-60 characters before it cuts a title off in search results. That is common guidance, not a guarantee, and the exact cut-off varies by device and by query. A headline that fits, says what the page is about and sounds specific gives a reader a clear reason to click.

How to use this Headline Analyzer

Paste or type a headline into the box above. The score updates as you type, so you can try a shorter version, add a number or swap a word and watch the score change. Read the score and the checks below it, then try one or two variants and keep the version that is clearest, not just the one with the highest number.

How the score works

The score is out of 100 and comes from five parts:

  • Length - 30 points. Headlines of 50-60 characters score best. Shorter or longer headlines score less.
  • Word count - 15 points. Headlines of 6-12 words score best.
  • Power and emotional words - 20 points. Headlines with at least one power word and at least one emotional word score best.
  • Number or question - 15 points. A headline with a number, a question word or a question mark scores best.
  • Clarity - 20 points. Points are taken off for all caps and for repeated exclamation marks.

This is RankForge's own checklist, not Google's algorithm. Google does not publish a headline score, and no tool can predict a ranking from a title alone. Use the score as a quick check for length and clarity, then judge the headline on whether it fits the page.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good headline length?

Aim for about 50 to 60 characters so the full title usually fits in search results. Shorter than 40 often wastes space, longer than 70 often gets cut off.

Does this tool store my headline?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

What are power words?

Short words that add feeling or urgency, like free, proven, simple, quick, and new. They help when they fit the topic, and they hurt when they sound like hype.

Should every headline have a number?

No. Numbers can help when the post really is a list or a set of steps. Forcing a number into a topic that is not a list usually reads worse.

Will a high score guarantee clicks?

No. A high score means the headline is clear, the right length, and specific. The topic, the search result around it, and the page itself still decide most clicks.
