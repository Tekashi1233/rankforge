What is an llms.txt file?


llms.txt is a plain-text file placed at the root of a website (https://yoursite.com/llms.txt). It gives large language models and AI answer engines clear, human-readable instructions about how they may use the site’s content. Think of it as a friendly counterpart to robots.txt that is written for AI systems rather than traditional crawlers.



Why llms.txt matters for GEO


Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is about making your content easy for AI systems to find, understand, and cite correctly. An llms.txt file helps by:


    

  • Stating whether AI systems are welcome to use your content
    • 

  • Providing a preferred way to cite or attribute your work
    • 

  • Pointing AI systems toward your most important or authoritative pages
    • 

  • Reducing the chance of incorrect or incomplete citations
    • 



It is still an emerging convention, but many sites and GEO tools already look for it.

Frequently asked questions


Is llms.txt required?
No. It is optional. Many sites perform well in AI answers without one. It simply gives AI systems clearer guidance.


Does every AI system read llms.txt?
Not yet. Support is growing, but it is not universal. It is still worth adding because the cost is near zero and the upside is clearer attribution.


Where should I put the file?
In the root of your domain, exactly like robots.txt: https://yoursite.com/llms.txt


Can I block AI training with this file?
You can state your preference. Whether a particular model respects it depends on that model’s policy. For stronger control, also review your robots.txt rules for AI crawlers.
