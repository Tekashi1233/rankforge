Backlink Checker

Discover every site that links to you, filter out toxic backlinks, and measure your domain’s real authority — all from your browser, with zero tracking and no account required.

What this tool will do

  • Show every referring domain and individual backlink in a clean, filterable list
  • Highlight toxic or spammy links you should consider disavowing
  • Estimate domain authority and link quality scores based on available signals
  • Export clean lists for outreach, reporting, or competitor research
  • Run 100% client-side — your URLs and data never leave your device

Unlike traditional checkers that require accounts and crawl the web, RankForge’s version will focus on the data you already have access to and keep everything private by design.
