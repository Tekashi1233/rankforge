About RankForge

RankForge is a collection of free SEO tools and plain-English guides for bloggers, students, small business owners and anyone who wants their pages to be easier to find on search engines.

Our mission

Good SEO tools should not be locked behind expensive subscriptions. We build simple, fast utilities that do one job well, and we explain the ideas behind them so you understand why each step matters.

What we offer

  • Free tools: meta tag generator, word counter, slug generator, robots.txt generator and keyword density checker.
  • Practical guides on keyword research, on-page SEO and technical SEO.

Your privacy

Our tools run entirely in your browser. The text you paste into a tool is processed on your device and is not uploaded to or stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How we make money

RankForge is free to use. We may show advertising to cover hosting and development costs. Ads never change the results our tools produce.
