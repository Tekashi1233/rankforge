About RankForge

RankForge is a collection of free SEO tools and plain-English guides for bloggers, students, small business owners and anyone who wants their pages to be easier to find on search engines.

Our mission

Good SEO tools should not be locked behind expensive subscriptions. We build simple, fast utilities that do one job well, and we explain the ideas behind them so you understand why each step matters.

What we offer

RankForge has 17 free tools, in four groups:

  • Technical SEO: Meta Tag Generator, XML Sitemap Generator, Robots.txt Generator, Schema Markup Generator, Heading Structure Checker, Hreflang Generator and Slug Generator.
  • Content and readability: Word Counter, Keyword Density Checker, Readability Score Checker, Headline Analyzer, Text Case Converter and Lorem Ipsum Generator.
  • AI search and GEO: GEO Score Checker, AI Readiness Checker and LLMs.txt Generator.
  • Everyday utilities: Password Generator.

Alongside the tools, the blog publishes plain-English guides on SEO basics, keyword research, on-page and technical SEO, and GEO: how answer engines read pages, schema markup, sitemaps, headings, readability, headlines, meta descriptions, passwords and more.

Built for the new search, too

Search is no longer just ten blue links. Answer engines now read your pages, pull out the facts, and quote them back to the user, sometimes without a click. RankForge covers that shift as well as the classic work. The GEO Score Checker grades a page for AI search, the AI Readiness Checker shows where a page is thin on structure and facts, and the LLMs.txt Generator builds the file that tells AI systems how to use and cite your content. The guides explain the same ideas in plain English, so you can see why a page that ranks well can still be invisible to an answer engine, and what to change.

Your privacy

Our tools run entirely in your browser. The text you paste into a tool is processed on your device and is not uploaded to or stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How we make money

RankForge is free to use. We may show advertising to cover hosting and development costs. Ads never change the results our tools produce.
