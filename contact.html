Contact Us

We read every message. Use the form or email us directly at thanosabdulla@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.

What to write about

Most messages fall into four groups, and all of them are welcome:

  • A bug: a tool gives the wrong answer, a page looks broken, or something does not load. Tell us which tool or page it is, what you typed or pasted, and what you expected to see. A screenshot helps.
  • A tool idea: a small SEO or writing check you wish existed. Tell us what it should do and when you would use it.
  • A correction: a guide says something that is out of date or wrong. Tell us which guide and which part, and we will check it.
  • Anything else: a question about a result, a suggestion for the site, or a press or partnership enquiry.

How the form works

The form on this page does not send your message through RankForge. When you click "Send message", it opens your own email app with the message filled in, addressed to thanosabdulla@gmail.com. You press Send in that app. That keeps the site simple: there is no account, no stored message, and nothing you type here is uploaded to our servers. If you would rather write directly, email thanosabdulla@gmail.com from any address.

Before you write

A quick check first can save you a wait. Most tool pages have a "How to use" section that explains what the tool counts and what its score means, and the blog guides cover the ideas behind the checks. If your question is about a result, tell us which tool you used and what you pasted in, so we can run the same check ourselves.
