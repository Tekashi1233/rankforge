How to use the Word & Character Counter

Paste your text to see its length instantly. Word counts help you match the depth of competing pages, character counts help with titles and social posts, and the reading time estimate shows whether your article suits your audience.

Frequently asked questions

Does the counter store my text?

No. Everything is counted in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

How is reading time calculated?

We divide the word count by about 230 words per minute, a typical adult reading speed.

How many words should a blog post have?

There is no fixed number. Cover the topic fully. Many helpful guides land between 800 and 2,000 words.
