What Is a Readability Score and Why It Matters for SEO

Quick answer: A readability score estimates how easy a piece of text is to understand. For most web content, aim for a Flesch Reading Ease score of 60–70 (about 8th–9th grade). Clear writing helps people stay longer and makes your pages easier for AI answer engines to use.

A readability score is a number that predicts how difficult a text will be for an average reader. The most common formulas are Flesch Reading Ease and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level. Both look at average sentence length and average syllables per word. You can check any page in seconds with our free Readability Score Checker.

How the scores work

Flesch Reading Ease produces a number from 0 to 100. Higher scores mean easier reading. Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level estimates the U.S. school grade needed to understand the text. A score of 8.0 means an average eighth-grader should follow it without trouble.

  • 90–100 — Very easy (5th grade)
  • 60–70 — Standard (8th–9th grade) — ideal for most websites
  • 30–50 — Difficult (college level)
  • 0–30 — Very difficult

Why readability matters for SEO

Google has never said it ranks pages by a readability formula. What it does reward is content that people find useful and easy to consume. Clear writing tends to improve:

  • Time on page
  • Bounce rate
  • Return visits
  • Shares and links

These user signals, combined with relevance and authority, influence rankings over time.

Why readability matters for GEO (AI search)

Generative engines such as Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and ChatGPT prefer text they can parse cleanly. Short sentences, clear structure, and everyday language make it easier for these systems to extract accurate answers and cite your page. Improving readability is one of the simplest GEO improvements you can make.

How to improve a low score

  • Break long sentences into two or three shorter ones.
  • Prefer common words over rare or academic ones.
  • Use active voice (“We tested the tool”) instead of passive (“The tool was tested”).
  • Keep paragraphs to three or four sentences.
  • Add headings and bullet lists so readers can scan.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good readability score for SEO?

Aim for a Flesch Reading Ease score between 60 and 70. That roughly matches an 8th–9th grade reading level and works well for most blog posts, product pages and guides.

Does Google use readability scores for ranking?

Google does not publish a direct readability ranking factor. However, clear writing improves user signals (time on page, bounce rate) and makes content easier for AI systems to parse and cite.

How do I improve a low readability score?

Use shorter sentences, simpler words, more active voice, and break long paragraphs. Avoid jargon when a plain word will do.

Is Flesch-Kincaid the same as Flesch Reading Ease?

They are related. Flesch Reading Ease gives a 0–100 score (higher = easier). Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level estimates the U.S. school grade needed to understand the text.

Conclusion

Readability is not a magic ranking lever, but it is a practical quality signal for both human readers and AI systems. Keep most web content around the 60–70 Flesch range, use the free checker to spot hard passages, and your pages will be easier to read, share and cite.
