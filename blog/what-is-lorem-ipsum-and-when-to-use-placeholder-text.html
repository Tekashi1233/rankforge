What Is Lorem Ipsum and When Should You Use Placeholder Text?

If you have ever seen a block of Latin-looking text that starts with "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet", you have met the most famous placeholder text in design. Designers, developers and writers use it to fill a layout before the real words are ready. This guide explains what it is, where it came from, and when to use it.

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is dummy text that looks like a normal paragraph but carries no meaning. Because the letters and word lengths resemble real language, it lets people judge fonts, spacing and layout without being distracted by readable content.

Where does it come from?

The text is derived from a work by the Roman writer Cicero, written in 45 BC, on the theory of ethics. Over time, the original passage was altered, scrambled and cut, so most lorem ipsum is not real, correct Latin. It has been used as standard filler text in printing and typesetting for centuries and moved into digital design later.

Why designers use it

  • Focus on design. Readable text makes people judge the words instead of the visual layout.
  • Realistic shape. Paragraphs look natural, unlike repeating "text here text here".
  • Speed. A generator fills a page in seconds, so you can test mockups, templates and themes quickly.

When placeholder text is useful

  • Building wireframes and mockups.
  • Testing a new website theme, template or email layout.
  • Checking how a design handles short and long paragraphs.
  • Demonstrating fonts and typography.

When it is a bad idea

Placeholder text hides real problems. Headlines that are too long, calls to action that do not fit and awkward line breaks only show up with real copy. Whenever possible, move to real content early in the process. Never publish a live page that still contains lorem ipsum.

Why you must replace it before launch

Pages with leftover filler look unfinished and untrustworthy to visitors. They also give search engines nothing useful to index, and large amounts of meaningless text can be treated as thin content. Before going live, search your site for the words "lorem" and "ipsum" to make sure none are left behind.
