What Is Lorem Ipsum and When Should You Use Placeholder Text?

If you have ever looked at a website template, you have probably seen paragraphs that begin with "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet". It looks like Latin, yet it does not quite make sense. This is placeholder text, used for centuries by printers and today by web designers. This guide explains what it is, why designers use it, and why it should never end up on your live website.

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is dummy text used to fill space in a layout when the real content is not ready. It looks like normal writing because its words and sentence lengths resemble real language, but it carries no meaning, so people judge the design instead of reading the text. It is widely used in website themes, mockups, brochures and templates.

Where does it come from?

The passage is a scrambled and altered version of a section from a work by the Roman writer Cicero, written in 45 BC. Printers began using jumbled Latin like this as sample text long ago, and it became a standard in digital design tools. Because it has been altered, many of the words are not proper Latin, which is why it reads strangely to Latin speakers.

Why designers use placeholder text

  • It keeps attention on the layout. Real sentences make people stop and read, which distracts from judging spacing, fonts and colours.
  • It looks natural. Repeating a single phrase such as "text goes here" gives an unrealistic picture of how paragraphs will look.
  • It saves time. A design can be built before the writing is finished.
  • It shows how text wraps. Different paragraph lengths help test how a layout handles real content.

When placeholder text is useful

  1. Building a website theme or template.
  2. Creating a mockup or wireframe to show a client.
  3. Testing fonts, line spacing and column widths.
  4. Checking how a design behaves with short and long paragraphs.
  5. Practising layout and design skills.

Why you should never leave it on a live site

This is the part that matters for SEO. Search engines want pages that offer real, useful content. A page full of lorem ipsum has nothing to offer a reader and is treated as low quality. Visitors who land on it leave quickly and lose trust in your site. It can also cause problems if you apply for advertising programs, because reviewers expect finished, original content. Before publishing any page, search it for the words "lorem ipsum" and make sure none remain. Look in headings, product descriptions, footers, image captions and any hidden sections of your theme.

How to generate placeholder text

You do not have to copy and paste the same block over and over. Our Lorem Ipsum Generator creates between one and ten paragraphs at a time. Each click gives slightly different text, which helps you test a layout with natural variety. Copy the result and paste it into your design.

Better alternatives in some cases

  • Draft real copy early. Even rough, real text shows far better how a design will work than placeholder text.
  • Use realistic sample content. For a tool or product page, write a few honest sample descriptions.
  • Write the content first. Designing around actual words avoids many layout surprises, such as headings that do not fit.

A pre-launch checklist

  1. Search every page for "lorem" and "ipsum".
  2. Replace placeholder images and their alt text too.
  3. Check that titles and descriptions are real, using the Meta Tag Generator.
  4. Read each page once as a visitor would.
  5. Review the basics again with our on-page SEO checklist.

Conclusion

Lorem ipsum is a handy design tool, useful for testing layouts before your real writing is ready. It must stay in the design stage. Use a generator while you build, replace every trace of it before you launch, and give your visitors and search engines real content that helps them.
